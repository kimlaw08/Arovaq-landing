Governance & Platform Guide

🎯 Mission & Vision

Mission: To build a non-custodial, peer-to-peer digital marketplace empowering creators and affiliates globally.

Vision: A transparent ecosystem backed by strict non-custodial rules and verifiable value.

🛡️ Non-Custodial Compliance (VASP Shield)

Arovaq operates on a strict rule: We do not hold customer funds. M-Pesa is used strictly as an immediate buying/checkout gateway. Once funds clear, they route instantly. Users independently utilize external on-ramp and off-ramp services.

📈 Affiliate Tiers & 50k Milestone Urgency

• Free Access Window: All affiliate commission tiers (2%, 10%, 20%, 40%, 50%) are 100% free for everyone until we hit 50,000 affiliates.

• Post-50k Lock: Once the 50k milestone is reached, the elite 40% and 50% tiers lock automatically. Access will require a paid VIP subscription (1,000 KES for 1 year or 1,500 KES for 2 years).

📜 General Rules, Regulations & T&Cs

• Product Protection: Access is governed by strict account/ID binding policies, EULAs, and Terms of Service (ToS) prohibiting unauthorized credential sharing or resale.

• Conduct: Fraudulent listings or manipulation result in immediate termination.

💡 How to Use, Wallets & Withdrawals

1. Buying: Use M-Pesa or card checkout. Instant split-pay routes funds directly.

2. Wallets: Connect your personal Web3 wallet via your profile to manage your earnings independently.

3. Withdrawals: Handled entirely through external on-ramp/off-ramp services since Arovaq never holds user balances.
